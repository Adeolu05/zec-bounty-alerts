# Zcash Improvement Proposals (ZIPs)

Welcome to the Zcash Improvement Proposals guide. Below you will find resources, guidelines, and a list of community projects that support ZIP development.

## Community Projects

- **ZecWallet** – A full‑featured Zcash wallet. ![ZecWallet Logo](/content-images/zecwallet.png)
- **ZecPages** – Documentation hosting for Zcash projects. ![ZecPages Logo](/content-images/zecpages.png)
- **ZipReader** – A web‑based viewer for ZIP files, useful for reviewing ZIP proposals directly in the browser. ![ZipReader Logo](/content-images/zipreader.png)
  
  Learn more at [zipreader.org](https://zipreader.org/).
